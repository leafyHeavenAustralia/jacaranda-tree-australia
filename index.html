Quick reference: Jacaranda mimosifolia
  • Introduced (South America); naturalised in parts of QLD and NSW. Not an Australian native.
  • Mature size 10–15 m high × 8–12 m wide. No true dwarf form of the standard blue jacaranda.
  • Blooms in spring: Sep–Oct in Brisbane, Oct–Nov in Sydney/Perth, Nov–Dec in Adelaide, late Nov–Dec in Melbourne.
  • First flowers: about 2–3 years (grafted) vs 5–8+ years (seed-grown).
  • Young trees are frost-tender; Melbourne is near the southern practical limit.
  • Litter: leaves, flowers and woody pods. Roots spread laterally, so allow space.
